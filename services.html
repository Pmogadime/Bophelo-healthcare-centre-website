Our Clinical Services

We provide a range of medical services tailored specifically to maternal and early childhood health.

1. Prenatal & Antenatal Care

Routine health assessments, ultrasound scans, blood tests, and maternal nutrition advice throughout pregnancy.

(Williams, 2026)

2. Postnatal Care & Infant Support

Post-delivery checkups for new mothers, infant health monitoring, growth tracking, and lactation guidance.

(Office On Women's Health, 2023)

3. Immunisation & Childhood Vaccines

Full immunisation programs according to national health standards to protect infants against infectious childhood diseases.

(UnityPoint Health, 2025)

4. Pediatric Health Screenings

Routine wellness checkups, early childhood developmental screening, and basic illness treatments for young children.

(World Health Organisation, 2023)
