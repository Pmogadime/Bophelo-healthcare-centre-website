About Our Facility

Bophelo Mother & Child Healthcare Centre is dedicated to delivering high-quality, accessible healthcare services tailored to maternal wellness and early childhood development in Limpopo.

Our Mission

To improve maternal and child health outcomes across regional communities by offering compassionate, evidence-based primary care services.

Our Vision

To establish an equitable primary healthcare environment where every mother and child receives optimal medical care and guidance.

Our Values

  • Compassion & Respect
  • Clinical Excellence
  • Community Health Engagement
  • Integrity & Trust
